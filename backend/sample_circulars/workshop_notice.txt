Subject: Hands-on Workshop on Generative AI and LLM Applications

The Department of AI&DS, in association with the IEEE Student Branch, is organising a one-day hands-on workshop on Generative AI and LLM Applications on 8 November 2026 from 9:30 AM to 4:30 PM in AI Lab 3.

The workshop is open to second-year, third-year and final-year students of all departments. Only 60 seats are available and will be allotted on a first-come, first-served basis.

Interested students must register online at https://abc.edu/genai-workshop on or before 5 November 2026. A registration fee of Rs. 200 is payable at the venue.

Participants should bring their own laptops with Python 3.10 installed. Certificates will be issued to all participants.

For more details, contact Prof. S. Karthik, Workshop Coordinator, at genai@abc.edu or 91234 56789.
