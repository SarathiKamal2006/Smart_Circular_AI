Subject: Internal Assessment Test II – Odd Semester 2026

Internal Assessment Test II for all second-year and third-year students will be conducted from 27 October to 31 October 2026. The tests will begin at 9:30 AM and end at 12:00 PM every day.

Students with attendance below 75% will not be permitted to write the test. Condonation requests must be submitted to the HoD on or before 22 October 2026.

For any clarification, contact the Examination Cell at examcell@abc.edu.
