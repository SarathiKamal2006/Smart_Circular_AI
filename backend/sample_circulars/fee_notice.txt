Subject: Payment of Semester Tuition Fee – Even Semester 2026-27

All students of the college are informed that the tuition fee for the even semester 2026-27 is payable online through the college ERP portal.

The tuition fee is Rs. 62,500 for regular students. Hostel students must additionally pay Rs. 38,000 towards hostel and mess charges.

The last date for fee payment is 20 November 2026. A late fine of Rs. 500 per day will be charged after the due date.

Students who face any problem with online payment should contact the Accounts Office between 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM or write to accounts@abc.edu. Payment receipts must be uploaded in the ERP and a printed copy submitted to the class advisor.
